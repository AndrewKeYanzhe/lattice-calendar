Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • Stored on your device. Calendar data, account details, and sign-in tokens are kept in local app storage.
  • Synced directly with Google. The app exchanges data with Google to retrieve your calendars and synchronize event changes.
  • No developer access. During normal use, your calendar data is not sent to the developer of Lattice Calendar or shared with third parties other than Google for synchronization.
  • You’re in control. Revoke access through your Google Account at any time. Clear the app’s data or uninstall it to remove locally cached information.

Read the full policy below for details and exceptions.

Lattice Calendar uses your Google data to show your calendar and sync the changes you make. Your calendar data is stored on your device and exchanged directly with Google.

This policy covers the Lattice Calendar HarmonyOS app and this website, maintained by Lattice Display Technologies.

Data the app accesses

  • Account information: your email address, display name, and profile picture, to identify the connected Google account in the app.
  • Calendar information: calendar names, identifiers, and colors, to display and select your calendars.
  • Event information: event titles, descriptions, locations, dates and times, recurrence details, and attendee information, to display your schedule and support event management.
  • Authorization information: access and refresh tokens issued by Google, to access your account without asking for your password or requiring sign-in for every sync.

Why Google access is requested

The app requests Google Calendar and Calendar Events permissions to read calendars and to read, create, update, and delete events as part of its calendar features. It also requests basic profile and email permissions to show which account is connected. Google presents the requested permissions when you sign in.

The app does not ask for or store your Google password. Sign-in takes place through Google in your device’s browser.

Storage and sharing

The app stores account information, authorization tokens, calendar lists, events, and settings in its local HarmonyOS app storage. Previously downloaded events are retained for offline access, and pending event changes are stored for synchronization.

The app communicates directly with Google over HTTPS. Lattice Calendar does not operate a backend that receives your calendar data. The app does not sell your Google data, share it with advertising services, or use it to train AI models. The developer does not receive your calendar data through normal use of the app.

If you choose to open an event location in Google Maps or open a link from an event, that destination receives the location query or link you choose to open and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Diagnostic messages may be written to device system logs. If you choose to send logs or screenshots for support, they may contain personal information; review them before sharing. Information you email to the developer is used to respond to your request.

Retention and removal

Local data remains in the app’s storage until it is overwritten or removed. Signing out should not be relied on to erase all previously cached events. To remove local app data, use your device’s app-data controls or uninstall the app; device backups, if enabled, are governed by your device and backup settings.

You can revoke Lattice Calendar’s access from your Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents future authorized sync but does not erase already downloaded data. Removing local app data does not delete events stored in Google Calendar. Event deletions made through the app may synchronize to Google.

Google API data use

Lattice Calendar’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This site is informational. It does not access your Google account, ask you to sign in, use analytics scripts, or set application cookies. The hosting provider may process request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the site. If hosted on GitHub Pages, see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests relating to information you have sent for support, contact Lattice Display Technologies at latticedisplaytechnologies@gmail.com. The developer cannot remotely erase data stored only on your device.
